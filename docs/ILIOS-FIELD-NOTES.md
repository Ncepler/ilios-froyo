# Ilios Froyo: field notes

Everything the 3D site needs to know about the real shop, compiled on 17 Oct 2026 from 31 public TikTok
videos (frame by frame), the Long Island Press and Newsday write-ups, and the existing build. Where something
was seen only once or is a best guess, it says so.

Shop: **Ilios Froyo**, 1355 Old Northern Blvd, Roslyn, NY 11576 (the former Yolo Yogurt space in Roslyn Village).
Owners Andrew and Sophia Quintero; Sophia grew up in Cyprus. Instagram @iliosfroyo. "Ilios" is Greek for sun.
Hours at opening: 5 to 10 pm, Tuesday to Sunday, closed Monday (they plan 2 to 10 pm once staffed).

---

## 1. The froyo

Greek-yogurt froyo, made in house daily from nonfat Greek yogurt plus kefir, sweetened with organic cane
sugar. Tart and vanilla are organic; the fruit flavors use 100% real fruit.

**Six flavors on three machines, three of them rotate monthly.** Seen on the machine signs (left to right along
the machine wall):

| Machine | Left handle | Right handle | Centre handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Organic Original Tart | Organic Original Tart | tart |
| 2 | Raspberry | Organic Vanilla Bean | twist |
| 3 | Sweet Harbor Coffee | Organic Vanilla Bean | twist |

- A hand-written "Banana" card was taped over one sign in one video (a rotation).
- Sweet Harbor Coffee is the cafe next door in Roslyn Village; the flavor is a collab.
- Colors as they look in the cup: tart is bright white with a faint blue cast; vanilla bean is warm ivory with
  fine black specks; raspberry is a dusty pink (#d99aa8 to #e3a6b4); coffee is a light latte tan (#c3a07c).
  A twist is two flavors spiralled together (vanilla + raspberry and vanilla + coffee are both common).

## 2. The machines

- Three **Electro Freeze** three-handle soft-serve machines (a "Tri-State, est. 1963, NY-NJ-CT" service sticker
  on each door) set side by side into **one long recessed frame** in the back wall: a raised limewash plaster
  frame around the whole row, plaster mullions between the machines, a warm LED cove glowing above.
- Each machine: stainless body, **black plastic freezer door** with three spouts and star caps, three chrome
  wire draw handles with black ball knobs, stainless drip tray with three oval drains.
- Over each machine, a white header sign: left flavor, gold sun face with ILIOS FROYO, right flavor (thin
  grey sans-serif caps).
- Cups stand in stacks right on the drip tray (big cups left, small cups right, sample cups in front).
- A black wire utility cart with stacks of cups sits at the end of the machine wall.
- Staff often pull for customers (black nitrile gloves). The cup is held up close to the spout and turned, so
  the froyo falls only a centimetre or two and lands as a soft ribbon that folds onto itself.

## 3. The cups

- White paper food containers with a rolled rim, gently tapered, a **printed cream square on the side with the
  gold/rose sun face and ILIOS FROYO** under it.
- **Two sizes plus sample cups.** The regular cup is nearly as tall as it is wide, not a squat bowl: from hands,
  spouts and drip-tray drains in several videos, about **9.6 cm across the rim, 7.4 cm across the base,
  8.4 cm tall** (a 16 oz food cup; height/rim about 0.87). The large one is taller than it is wide
  (about 11.5 cm rim, 12 cm tall, ~32 oz). Sample cups are 2 oz souffle cups.
- Spoons: white plastic. Napkins: white.
- The site models the regular cup.

## 4. The topping bar

**Furniture:** one long counter of light grey veined quartz, **low glass sneeze guard** with teal glass edges
(an L of glass: a front panel and a top shelf). Bowls sit in **two staggered rows** (a front row against the
glass, a back row behind), so you reach over the front row for the back. Behind the bar on the staff side:
light oak base cabinets, a sink, a shelf of white Greek vases and jugs, brass globe sconces, and raised ILIOS
FROYO letters on the back wall. A curved ceiling soffit with downlights follows the counter.

**Serveware:** white organic, wavy-rimmed bowls (most items), round white hobnail (beaded) bowls for the
strawberries and coconut, a wood-rimmed rectangular tray for the raspberry sauce, wooden spoons for fruit
and sprinkles, stainless tongs for anything chunky, a clear plastic scoop for the M&M's, a white Chinese soup
spoon for the chocolate jimmies, an ice-cream scoop (disher) for cookie dough.

**Labels:** fruit labels are white cards standing in small travertine blocks; candy labels are tiny black
chalkboard flags on picks.

### Order along the line

You fill your cup at the machines, then walk the bar back towards the front. In order (seen in several
walk-throughs; the bar rotates, so not every item is out every day):

**Fresh fruit and sauce** (self serve)
1. Strawberries, diced (big hobnail bowl, wooden spoon)
2. Golden mango, diced
3. Granola (nut free, gluten free)
4. Dates, chopped Medjool (tongs)
5. Fresh figs, quartered (tongs)
6. Raspberry sauce (wood tray, ladle)
7. Kiwi, diced
8. Blueberries
9. Green grapes
10. Honeydew melon balls
11. Shredded coconut (hobnail bowl)
12. Pineapple cubes (seen on some days; may be a second mango bowl)

**Candy and crunch** (self serve)
13. Gummy bears
14. M&M's
15. Rainbow sprinkles (nonpareils and jimmies mix)
16. Brownie bites
17. Rainbow cookies (NY tricolor almond cookies, sliced)
18. Chocolate chips
19. Chocolate sprinkles (jimmies)
20. Rice Krispies treat cubes
21. Oreo crumble
22. Mini chocolate chip cookies
23. Biscoff cookies, broken
24. Cookie dough (scoop)
25. Sour rainbow strips, Swedish Fish and gummy worms (one bowl or two)
26. Strawberry marshmallow / mochi cubes (pink and white; seen once, name not legible)
27. Chocolate bar chunks (Kinder-style)

**The warm station** (served by staff behind the glass, at the end of the bar, near the register)
White ceramic non-stick skillets with blond wooden handles, sitting on a white induction warming plate
("Laudlife" printed on it). Brown chunky lettering on the front glass names them (HONEY, CINNAMON ROLL, ...).
28. **Pistachio kataifi** (the signature). A thick, textured khaki-green filling: pistachio cream with toasted
    shredded kataifi folded through, like a Dubai-chocolate filling. It **sits in a skillet** and is scooped
    out with a disher or spoon in a soft heavy dollop that slumps a little and holds. It is never a thin
    drizzle. A second skillet of plain toasted kataifi crumble sits beside it on some days.
29. **Nutella**, warm, ladled and poured from a spoon in a glossy ribbon.
30. **Biscoff cookie butter**, warm, caramel colored, poured from a spoon.
31. **Cinnamon roll**: mini cinnamon-roll slices (little spirals) soaking in a skillet of warm cream-cheese
    icing. Staff spoon one or two rolls plus a pour of icing; the icing glues them down onto the froyo.
32. **Honey** in a glass drizzler jar with a steel top, on the customer side of the glass.
33. **Extra virgin olive oil + flaky sea salt** (poured from a painted Greek ceramic jug; a fan favorite).

Typical cup: $12 to $16, paid by weight at the register.

## 5. The room and the walk-through

Walking in from the street:

1. **Outside:** a historic white Victorian storefront on Old Northern Blvd: porch with white columns and
   carved brackets, brick sidewalk, double glass doors painted slate grey with a brass PUSH plate.
2. **Inside, left:** the seating lounge along the front windows: tall white double-hung windows, cream boucle
   barrel chairs, round white marble tables on turned walnut pedestals, potted olive trees, a money tree.
3. **Inside, right:** the register counter, clad in pale stone, with a POS screen and scale, a clamshell dish
   of foil chocolates and a candle. Behind it the **gold 3D sun face with ILIOS FROYO** on the limewash wall.
   A mirrored A-frame sign by it reads "FROYO IS SO F*CKING BACK".
4. **Down the room:** the long topping counter runs from the register towards the back under a curved
   soffit. You walk past it (it is on your right going in).
5. **The back wall:** the three machines in their framed recess, cup cart at the end.
6. Then you turn around: cup in hand, you walk **back up the bar** adding toppings (fruit first, then candy),
   hand it over the glass at the warm station, and pay at the register by the door.

**Palette:** warm ivory and beige limewash walls, pale stone floor tiles, light grey veined quartz counters,
light oak cabinetry, walnut table bases, cream boucle, brushed gold (the sun), touches of teal in the glass
edges, olive green from the trees. Lighting is warm (2700 K) downlights, globe sconces and LED coves.
Music: loud European summer playlist, "like a club".

## 6. How the site uses this

- **Hero (the pour):** machine 3 (Sweet Harbor Coffee / twist / Organic Vanilla Bean), the cup held up to the
  spout, a soft ribbon that falls a short way and folds into coils.
- **Cup:** the regular 16 oz size above.
- **Top it off:** you scroll down the bar from the machine end. The cup sits on the counter in front of the
  bar and the line slides past it to the left as you scroll (so you move right, down the line). Every bowl is
  clickable where it sits; a spoon (or tongs, scoop, disher, ladle, as in the shop) lifts a portion from that
  bowl, carries it over the cup and tips it in. Items land in the order you click them and stay put.
  The warm station comes last, then the register.
