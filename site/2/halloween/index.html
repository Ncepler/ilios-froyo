The signature

Pistachio kataifi, bubbling warm.

Pistachio cream with toasted, shredded kataifi folded through, kept warm at the end of the bar. Officially it lives in a skillet; in October we call it the cauldron. It goes on in one soft, heavy dollop. Never a drizzle.

How it works

Pull. Top. Weigh.

Self serve, the way froyo should be. Staff are always happy to pour for you, costume or not.

  1. 01

    Pull

    Grab a cup and pull your own from six handles. Can't choose? The middle handle twists two together.

  2. 02

    Top

    Walk the bar back toward the door: fresh fruit first, then trick-or-treat candy, then the cauldron behind the glass.

  3. 03

    Weigh

    Pay by weight at the register, under the gold sun. Have as much, or as little, as you like. No tricks.

Our story

Ilios is Greek for sun.

Ilios is a family shop, run by Andrew and Sophia Quintero. Sophia grew up in Cyprus, and the froyo tastes like it: thick, tangy, properly Greek.

We make it in house every day from nonfat Greek yogurt and kefir, sweeten it with organic cane sugar, and use real fruit in every fruit flavor. The gold sun is on every cup, over every machine, and on the wall when you walk in.

Come October, the sun clocks out early. We don't: Tuesday to Sunday, 5 to 10 pm.

Good questions

Before you creep by.

Can I come in costume?

Please do. Halloween falls on a Saturday this year, and we're open our usual 5 to 10 pm.

What makes it Greek froyo?

We make it from nonfat Greek yogurt plus kefir, so it's thicker and tangier than most froyo, sweetened with organic cane sugar. The tart and vanilla bean are organic, and the fruit flavors use real fruit.

How does pricing work?

Fill your cup, add whatever you like from the bar, and pay by weight at the register.

Do the flavors change?

Yes. Three of the six handles rotate every month. This page and our Instagram always show what's pouring.

What's in the cauldron?

Pistachio kataifi, Nutella, Biscoff cookie butter and cinnamon rolls in cream-cheese icing, all kept warm behind the glass. Ask and we'll spoon them on.

Anything nut free or gluten free?

Our granola is nut free and gluten free. Everything shares one bar, so if you have a serious allergy, talk to us before you build your cup.

Do I have to pull my own?

It's self serve, but never a test. Our team is happy to pour for you.
