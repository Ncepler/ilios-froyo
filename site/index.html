    The name

    Named for the sun.

    Ilios is the Greek word for sun. It's the gold face on every cup, on the sign over the machines, and on the wall when you walk in.

    Ήλιοςee-lee-os · the sun

    On the machines

    Pull one. Or twist two.

    Three machines, six flavors, made in house every day from Greek yogurt and kefir and sweetened with organic cane sugar. The tart and the vanilla are organic; the fruit flavors are made with real fruit.

    Organic Original Tart

    The classic: bright, clean and properly tangy.

    Organic Vanilla Bean

    Real vanilla bean, specks and all. On two machines, for twists.

    Raspberry

    Made with real raspberries. Twist it with the vanilla bean.

    Sweet Harbor Coffee

    A coffee froyo, named for Sweet Harbor Coffee in Roslyn Village.

    Rotating flavors

    Three of the six change every month. Banana has made an appearance.

    The machines change with the month. Ask at the counter, or check @iliosfroyo for what's pouring now.
